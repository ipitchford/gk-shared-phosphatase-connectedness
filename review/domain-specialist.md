## Findings

No required domain correction identified.

1. **Network correspondence is correct.** The two-layer common-phosphatase architecture, rate order, and species order agree with Kaihnsa–Wang equation (34). The first-layer substrate total correctly includes the second-layer kinase complex v2. Four conservation relations with independent pivots and rank six give the claimed sixteen-dimensional physical parameter space. The article's Conjecture 4.3 is about full rates and totals; the proposed theorem is a contradiction of that conjecture, not of its two-component upper bound. The reported threshold-denominator typo is present in the source. [Kaihnsa–Wang v1, Section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25456v1#S4).

2. **Degree-theorem hypotheses and sign are adequately handled.** The manuscript checks a strictly positive conservation relation and excludes boundary equilibria whenever all totals are positive. Its conservation rows replace the first four Jacobian rows in exactly the convention required by the cited theorem, and rank six makes the sign prefactor positive. Both the negative-critical implication and the contrapositive yielding a nonpositive-critical equilibrium are legitimate. In particular, the manuscript does not silently promote the necessary nonpositive condition to a sufficient condition at zero. The explicit ray starts above an already multistationary physical parameter, which avoids that error. [Telek–Feliu, Theorem 1 and Lemmas 9–10](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010970).

3. **Biochemical and topological scope are calibrated.** Mass-action complexes are retained; this is not an unannounced Michaelis–Menten approximation. The full physical projection permits rates and totals to vary, and overlap of different equilibrium lifts legitimately connects their images. Negative-critical is not equated with stable; multistationarity is not equated with bistability. The manuscript expressly excludes physiological interpretation of the extreme witness concentrations and fixed-slice connectivity.

4. **Prior architecture and method attribution is fair.** Feliu–Wiuf motif (k) has the non-layer-specific phosphatase cascade, with activated first-layer substrate catalysing the second layer; its text establishes multistationarity and uses elimination/response functions. The manuscript credits this antecedent and confines its contribution to physical-region topology. [Feliu–Wiuf, Sections 2 and 4](https://web.math.ku.dk/~pbx512/journalWiuf/jRSocInterface9.pdf).

5. **Optional improvement:** add Giaroli–Bihan–Dickenstein to the manuscript bibliography and briefly note its explicit multistationarity-region work for this cascade family, as already recorded in the bundle's prior-art document. This would improve visibility of an antecedent currently available only in accompanying material. It is not a missing premise of this proof.
